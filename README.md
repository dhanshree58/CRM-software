# CRM Software

A full-stack Customer Relationship Management (CRM) application designed to manage customers, orders, invoices, deliveries, products, and business reports in one place.

## Features
- Customer and product management
- Order creation and tracking
- Invoice generation and printing
- Delivery management
- Dashboard with business insights
- Reports and sales tracking

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js, Vite
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL


## Purpose
To simplify business operations, improve order management, and maintain customer information efficiently through a centralized system.
